International Travel Policy
Employees traveling internationally must obtain manager approval at least two weeks before departure.
The company covers airfare, hotel, and daily allowances according to the travel policy.
Travelers must submit expense reports within 15 days after returning.

Domestic Travel Policy
Domestic travel only requires manager approval.
Mileage reimbursement follows company guidelines.

Annual Leave
Employees receive 15 days of annual leave.
Unused leave cannot be carried over.
